class pypufferblow.calls.Calls(options: CallsOptions)[source]#

Bases: object

1:1 voice calls and the identity keys that make them end-to-end encrypted.

The instance only relays the set-up messages of a call (and stores public keys); the audio, the signatures and the encryption are the clients’ business. A bot has no use for the media itself, but can ring, answer and relay signals, which is what this wraps.

Answer a call you were rung for. Returns {status: "accepted", ice_servers}.

end(call_id: str) → dict[source]#

End a call.

invite(callee_id: str) → dict[source]#

Ring someone. Returns {call_id, status, ice_servers, ...}.

Raises:

  • UserNotFound – There is no such user.

  • CallBusy – You are already on a call, or rang this person a moment ago. (Whether the other person is on a call is not reported: they are not rung and the call rings out.)

  • CallRefused – Either of you has blocked the other, or you tried to call yourself.

register_identity_key(public_key: dict, label: str | None = None) → dict[source]#

Register the public half of a P-256 signing key (a JWK with kty, crv, x, y). Returns {created, key}. Idempotent. Raises CallRefused for a key that is invalid, a private key, held by another account, or when you already have 10.

Relay one set-up message to the other person: offer, answer, candidate or relay. Only once the call has been accepted; the payload may weigh at most 32 KB.
